# kb

Every bridge has this feat, so there is always something to dive from. Dive
on it to maintain the knowledge base -- fix what drifted from the code, link
what is unlinked, retire what is stale -- and to define work: new feats,
decks and notes. It never closes.

`nosedive seed` writes it once, scoped to the bridge itself, and links it
from the backlog as `zerostar.feat`; after that it is the bridge's to edit.
A dive on it hydrates the bridge, and what the dive writes there publishes
when it lands.

# Feats

A feat records a goal we want to accomplish. Its `kind: feat` KB document can
declare the repositories the work may touch and own multiple bounded dives as
the work is planned, implemented, reviewed, and resumed.

The feat is the "why"; a [dive](./00000000-0000-749c-844b-2318117bffb0.md) is
one concrete attempt at moving toward its goal.

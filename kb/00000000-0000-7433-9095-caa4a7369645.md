# Feats

A feat is the durable thing we are trying to accomplish. It is represented by
a `kind: feat` KB document, can declare the repositories it may touch, and can
own multiple bounded dives as the work is implemented, reviewed, and resumed.

The feat is the “why”; a dive is one concrete attempt at moving that goal
forward.

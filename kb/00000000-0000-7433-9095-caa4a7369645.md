# Feats

A feat is the durable thing we are trying to accomplish -- what we'll build. It
is represented by a `kind: feat` KB document, can declare the repositories it
may touch, and can own multiple bounded dives as the work is implemented,
reviewed, and resumed.

The feat is the "why"; a [dive](./00000000-0000-749c-844b-2318117bffb0.md) is
one concrete attempt at moving toward its goal.

# Dives

A dive is a small enough slice of work to enable
[TBD](https://trunkbaseddevelopment.com/). Its KB document records who's on it
right now, pinned repository scopes, progress, patches, and handoff context.

A workspace can support a single in-flight dive at a time. This encourages
agents (and humans) to pack work-in-progress before switching contexts, to make
pickup easier for the next diver.

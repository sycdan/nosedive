# Pack

`pack` is the pause or handoff action. It captures current work from every
writable scoped repository and linked bridge KB docs as patch artifacts,
records the state in the dive, and leaves the work resumable without depending
on one machine or chat window.

A pack puts the work down for review or continuation. It does not declare the
dive complete; [`land`](./00000000-0000-70db-810d-748b23bd9e7c.md) is the
completion verb.

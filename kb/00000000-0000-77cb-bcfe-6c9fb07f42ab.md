# Dive

A dive is recorded on a feat: `echo <brief> | nosedive crud dive --feat
<feat> [--title <title>] <gist...>`. Crud hands it to `record.dive`'s own
code, which names it `<feat>.<last-6>`, inherits the feat's scopes and work
branches, pins each at origin's head, and links it from the feat as
`planned.dive`. It is written where the kind is: the bridge with no dive, and
on a dive that scopes the bridge, its `__self` checkout, published by land.
Recording claims nothing; `jump` does.

Its meta is nosedive's: `feat`, the `diver` and `packer` that hold it, and
`effort`, which older dives name instead of `feat`. A closed dive becomes a
memo, whose meta is open.

# Bridge

A plain notes repo becomes a `bridge` once nosedive is seeded into it: one repo
that holds all necessary context and drives the work across your other repos. It
is a special `kind: repo` that is referenced as the `bridge` in
`.nosedive/config.yaml` and hydrated into `<nosedive-workspace>/__self` when a
dive needs to work on it.

Everything Nosedive tracks lives inside a bridge's
[kb](00000000-0000-7bee-b718-0c6abe48ee4f.md):
[repos](00000000-0000-7a04-a8f4-6f75484220cb.md),
[feats](00000000-0000-7433-9095-caa4a7369645.md),
[dives](00000000-0000-749c-844b-2318117bffb0.md),
[gates](00000000-0000-76cb-a899-f8e90db8ca5c.md), and
[memos](00000000-0000-70fd-9a8c-5b0f34e248e2.md).

If you have met the term metarepo, a bridge is one -- but "metarepo" describes
a shape (one repo governing others) without saying what it is *for*. A bridge
is specifically for holding context and driving work: it is where an
agent finds out what to do next and how to prove it is done.

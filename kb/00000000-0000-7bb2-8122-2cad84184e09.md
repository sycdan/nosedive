# Memo

The one shipped kind with an open schema. Memos carry whatever their writer
needed -- a closed dive keeps `feat` and `diver`, a note keeps nothing -- so
declaring their keys would turn every older memo into an error the next time
it is edited. A memo becomes a feat, a dive or a deck's entry by how other
docs link it, not by its kind.

`crud memo <gist>` mints one.

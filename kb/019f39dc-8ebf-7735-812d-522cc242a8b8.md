# Slug chain

The **slug chain** of an effort is its slug followed by its ancestors,
**leaf-first**, joined by `.`:

```text
<effort-slug>.<parent-slug>.<grandparent-slug>...
```

A top-level effort's chain is just its own slug.

## Why leaf-first

The effort under work is the first token — matching how you target it,
narrowest scope first, like `subdomain.example.com`. The trade-off: siblings
under one parent don't sort together in a branch/worktree listing. We optimize
for "what am I working on" over parent-grouping.

## Where it's used

- **Session dir** — `<slug-chain>.<uuid>`. The uuid suffix lets multiple
  concurrent sessions target the same effort.
- **Effort branch** — `nosedive/effort/<slug-chain>`. The single canonical
  topic branch per effort; no uuid, since many sessions merge into one.

## Separator

`.` is legal in git refs: no `..`, no trailing `.`, no `.lock` component. A
session branch (`nosedive/session/<slug-chain>.<uuid>`) therefore carries
multiple dots without issue.

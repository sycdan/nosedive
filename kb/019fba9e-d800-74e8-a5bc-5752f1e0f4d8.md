# Dump backlog

Renders the body of the bridge KB memo configured as `backlog:` in
`.nosedive/config.yaml`.

Use [`update-backlog`](019fbe0d-c000-7d11-86f2-8f90f227163a.md) to regenerate
that memo from bridge KB effort docs.

At L1, `backlog:` is the UUID of the memo that indexes current work. The L0
directory walker remains available as
[`dump-backlog@0`](019fadf5-e08e-7f5e-b7d0-3b654b828512.md) for legacy bridges.

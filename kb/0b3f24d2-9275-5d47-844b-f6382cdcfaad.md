# Nuke

Destructive, so it fails unless `--config` is provided.

Removes `.nosediverc`, `.nosedive/config.yaml`, `.nosedive.local.yaml`, and
`.nosedive/.gitignore` when present, and reports how many files it removed.

Also removes nosedive-managed config exclude blocks from Git's local
`.git/info/exclude`, including the older package-foundation exclude block.

Bridge kb, backlog, and workspace contents are left alone.

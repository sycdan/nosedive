# Plan

`plan` is the planning entrypoint between `preflight` and `jump`. Its stdout is
a prompt, so it accepts the global `--exec` flag and uses the bridge's agent
effort ladder when asked to execute the prompt.

It prints pilot identity, the configured backlog memo, and `<context>` verbatim.
The directive at the end tells the planning agent to help the pilot choose which
feat to plan without choosing for them or starting work.

Once the pilot chooses, the agent inspects the feat and its scoped repositories
and breaks the work into vertical slices at logical seams. Each slice must:

- deliver one end-to-end behavior;
- represent no more than half a day's work from the planner's perspective;
- be recorded under the feat with `record.dive`, including its gist, title, and
  write-once brief;
- remain unclaimed and unstarted;
- link a runnable gate with `rel: land.gate`;
- have a test that fails against the feat's current scoped refs for the intended
  reason and states what must pass before the dive can land.

The planning agent stops after recording the slices, linking their gates, and
confirming the tests fail. It never calls `jump` or implements a planned dive.

`into` is retained as a deprecated compatibility route to this prompt.

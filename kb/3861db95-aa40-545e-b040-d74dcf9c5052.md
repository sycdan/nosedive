# dive

The dive commands of the record family, which is deprecated, under one name.
Both forms read stdin, because `npx` cuts a multi-line argument at its first
newline.

## dive <feat> <gist...>

```sh
echo "What to do, and how to tell it is done." | nosedive dive helm Add the note button
```

Records a dive on the feat -- a quid, a kb path, or a name -- with the words
after it as its gist and stdin as its brief, and commits it, exactly as
`record.dive --feat <feat> --gist <gist> --brief -` does. `--title` sets the
heading, which defaults to the gist. `nosedive jump` picks it up.

## dive --log[:<event>] -

```sh
echo "Built the form; tests pass." | nosedive dive --log:built -
```

Appends a timestamped section to the active dive, exactly as
`append-log.dive` does; `<event>` becomes the section's label.

`record.dive` and `append-log.dive` keep working, unchanged, for everything
`dive` does not cover yet -- patching a dive, scopes, takeover.

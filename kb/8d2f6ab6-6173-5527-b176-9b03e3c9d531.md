# Test

Runs gates and reports what held. Every selected gate runs, in sequence, each
in its own process; output streams as it is produced rather than after the run.

## Selecting gates

With no arguments, runs the gates the active dive claims through its own
`rel: land.gate` links, and nothing else. That is the form to reach for while
working: the checks for what is being changed, not everything that guards the
repository.

`--full` widens the selection to every gate reachable from the dive, its feat
and its scoped repos -- exactly the set `land` would run, so a clean
`test --full` is a truthful preview of a land rather than a similar one.

`<gate-id>` runs one gate by UUID, whatever it is attached to.

A dive gate needs no `gate-height`. Height exists to sequence a land; a
dive-sized set is small enough that discovery order is the honest order.

## Requires a dive

Every form except a named gate needs a dive on deck, and errors with
`no-active-dive.error` without one. There is nothing to select from otherwise:
the selection roots *are* the dive, its feat and its scoped repos.

Naming a gate is the exception, because the gate is already chosen.

## Reporting

A failing gate does not stop the run. Every selected gate runs, every failure
is named at the end, and the command exits 1 -- "these three are broken" is
actionable where "something is broken" only earns another run.

A gate document must have a gate kind and a bridge-relative `meta.test-script`
exporting `run(ctx)`. Hydrated workspace repositories are provided through
`ctx.repos`, and the active dive id through `ctx.diveId`.

A dive that links no gates is reported and exits non-zero. Zero gates is the
one verdict a test command must never call success: it would teach a pilot that
green means checked when it means unchecked.
